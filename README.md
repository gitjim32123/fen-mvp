FEN! - Fast Earn Nearby

Technology Bringing Communities Back Together

Years ago, if you needed help, you would ask a neighbour.

Today, most people turn to the internet, yet many of us barely know the people living around us.

FEN combines the power of technology with the strength of local communities.

Whether it’s walking a dog, moving furniture, collecting a parcel, or helping with everyday tasks, FEN helps neighbours connect, support one another, earn money, and build stronger communities.

We’re not just creating another jobs app.

We’re helping neighbours help neighbours.
